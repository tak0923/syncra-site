SYNCRA Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 Last updated: October 18, 2026 Operator: SYNCRA Support Office (the "Operator") Contact: SYNCRA official LINE (https://lin.ee/VHWRBAL)

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the Operator handles users' information in SYNCRA, a collaboration app for Logic Pro (the Mac app, the iPhone and iPad app and SYNCRA Monitor; together, the "Service").

2. Information we collect and why

Information Details Purpose
Account information If you sign in with Apple: your Apple identifier and email address (Apple's relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"), display name, @ID, profile picture Sign-in, identifying you, showing members, invitations
Production data Audio files, versions (history) of Logic projects, timeline positions and notes (shared notes, done/undone state, etc.) Providing the collaboration features
Communication Messages and attachments (images, videos, files) Providing messaging between members
Device information Device ID, push notification token, notification settings, connected devices (device links) Delivering notifications, linking your devices
Usage status Online status (presence), synchronization data such as playhead position Sharing status during collaboration
Activity records Audit records such as adding or removing members Preventing misuse, handling problems
Contacts feature Friend relationships (@ID) you add Friends feature

Blocks Who blocked whom So that a blocked person's messages, invitations and notifications are not delivered
Subscription and billing information Plan, period, renewal date, whether cancellation is scheduled, and subscription status. Paddle subscription and customer IDs, App Store transaction IDs, and a record of notifications received from payment providers. Card numbers and similar payment data are processed by the payment provider; the Operator does not receive them Providing paid features, preventing duplicate subscriptions, handling inquiries
Mobile number match value A value computed from the mobile number verified by SMS (a keyed hash; the number itself is not stored), the verification time, and the number of SMS sent per day Preventing abuse of the invite discount (one account and one use per number)

Information we do not collect: location, your device's address book, health information, browsing history. We do not track you for advertising, and we do not send data to analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera: only to scan the QR code shown by SYNCRA on your Mac. Video is never saved or sent.
  • Photos (add only): only when you choose to save a photo or video you received in a message. We never read your photo library.
  • Local network: to connect a Mac with an iPhone or iPad/Monitor on the same network (Bonjour).

4. Disclosure to third parties and processors

The Operator entrusts the storage and processing of data to the following providers, only to the extent necessary to provide the Service. They handle data only on the Operator's instructions. - Supabase, Inc. (authentication, database, functions) - Cloudflare, Inc. (R2: storage of audio and attachments) - Apple Inc. (sign-in, push notification delivery, in-app purchase and App Store Server Notifications) - Paddle.com Market Ltd (seller of purchases in the Mac app. Paddle collects your name, email address, country and payment information under its own privacy policy; the Operator is notified of the subscription status and the subscription and customer IDs) - Twilio Inc. (mobile number verification by SMS; the number is sent to Twilio for verification)

Except where required by law, we do not provide your information to any third party other than the above without your consent.

5. Where data is stored (transfers abroad)

Data may be stored on servers in Japan, the United States or other countries operated by the providers listed in section 4. By using the Service you acknowledge that your data may be stored outside your country.

6. Retention and deletion

  • When you delete a project or file, we keep it for 30 days so it can be restored, and then delete it permanently.
  • Deleting your account: you can delete your account at any time from Settings in the iPhone or iPad app or the Account screen on the Mac. When you do:
  • Deleted immediately: your name, picture and @ID; friends; block information; notification destinations; registered devices; messages, photos and videos you sent in other people's projects.
  • Made unavailable immediately, deleted permanently after 30 days: projects you own (including audio files; other members also lose access).
  • Kept: audio, notes and versions you left in other people's projects (they are part of a joint work; your name is no longer shown), and report records (for safety, kept for 3 years).
  • If you signed in with Apple, deleting from the iPhone or iPad app also revokes the link with Apple.
  • When a paid plan ends, your data is not deleted (you can still view and download it and delete your account).
  • If you used only the free trial and did not subscribe, the cloud projects you created during the trial are deleted 30 days after the trial ends.
  • Subscription records are kept after a subscription ends for inquiries, abuse prevention and accounting records, for 7 years. Records of notifications from payment providers are deleted after 90 days.
  • Mobile number match values and invite records are kept after account deletion for abuse prevention, for 3 years.

7. Your rights (access, correction, deletion, etc.)

To request disclosure, correction, suspension of use or deletion under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information, contact us via SYNCRA official LINE (https://lin.ee/VHWRBAL). We will confirm your identity and respond within a reasonable period. You can withdraw consent by deleting your account or contacting us.

8. Security

Communication is encrypted (HTTPS), and access to data is limited by permissions (project membership). No system is completely secure, however.

9. Children

The Service is intended for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please use it with your parent or guardian's consent. If we learn that we hold information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app or on the web.
