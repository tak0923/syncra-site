01 / Listen

Hear Logic in your hand

SYNCRA Monitor on Logic Pro's Stereo Out streams uncompressed 32-bit float audio to your iPhone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi. Jump anywhere from the waveform, skip 10 seconds, return to LIVE in a tap, switch stereo / mono / left / right and see the latency.

02 / Loop

Store three loops

Draw a range on the waveform and store it as A, B or C; recall it with a tap. Loops follow Logic's cycle, so you can repeat a section without touching your Mac.

FAQ

What is SYNCRA?

A collaboration app that lets you listen to the Logic Pro project on your Mac from an iPhone or iPad and review and fix it together.

Can I use it on iPhone or iPad alone?

To hear audio you need SYNCRA for Mac (macOS 14 or later) and Logic Pro.

I cannot hear anything

Check that SYNCRA Monitor is on Logic's Stereo Out, that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, and that SYNCRA is allowed under Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on the iPhone or iPad.

Does the free trial turn into a paid plan?

No. It is 7 days, once per person, up to 1 GB. Cloud projects from an unconverted trial are deleted 30 days after it ends.

Cancelling and refunds

Cancelling stops future renewal charges. On iPhone and iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. See Seller & Refunds.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Delete Account.
