Features

A new flow for reviewing your mix.

No need to crowd around the Mac. Hear the same audio, point at the same spot and settle fixes on the spot.

Hear Logic on iPhone and iPad

SYNCRA Monitor, inserted in Logic Pro, streams uncompressed 32-bit float audio to your iPhone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi. Jump anywhere from the full waveform, skip 10 seconds, return to LIVE in a tap. Stereo / mono / left / right and latency display.

Store three loops

Draw a range on the waveform and store it as A, B or C; recall it with a tap. Loops follow Logic's Cycle, so you can repeat a section without touching the Mac.

Solo any Track

Switch Track solo from your iPhone or iPad. Soloed Tracks are overlaid on the waveform in color.

Shared Notes right where you hear it

Add a Shared Note at the playback position in one tap, by Bar and Beat or by minutes and seconds. Tap a note or message and Logic's Playhead on your Mac jumps there.

Talk with your Members

Messages and Shared Notes with photo and video attachments. Notes can be checklists so nothing to fix is missed. See who is Online and where they are listening.

Available in 7 languages

Japanese, English, German, French, Spanish, Korean and Portuguese (Brazil), using Logic Pro's own terms in each language.

How to use

Every feature, on the real screens

Tap an item in the moving strip above to jump to that feature.

01

Hear Logic on iPhone and iPad

  1. In Logic Pro on the Mac, insert SYNCRA Monitor on Stereo Out.
  2. Open SYNCRA on the iPhone or iPad and choose the Mac on the same Wi-Fi (QR code or 6-digit code the first time).
  3. When LIVE lights up, you hear Logic's audio as it is.

Audio arrives as uncompressed 32-bit float. Stereo / mono / left / right, the output device and latency are in the settings at the bottom.

02

Jump anywhere on the full waveform

  1. The whole song's waveform is shown. Drag to a spot and Logic's Playhead on the Mac moves there.
  2. Bar and Beat as well as minutes and seconds are shown, so positions are easy to share.
  3. Tap LIVE to return to where Logic is playing now.

Move around the song from your iPhone or iPad without touching the Mac.

03

Store loops as A, B and C

  1. Drag on the waveform to set the range you want to repeat.
  2. Store the range on the A, B or C button; a tap recalls it.
  3. Turn on Loop and it repeats in sync with Logic's Cycle.

Switch between three spots, such as the pre-chorus, a break and the chorus.

04

Control Logic from iPhone and iPad

  1. The big centre button plays and stops.
  2. The 10 buttons skip 10 seconds back or forward.
  3. Check the position on the waveform and the Bar / Beat display.

Keep your instrument in hand and control playback from where you sit.

05

Solo Tracks, shown in colour

  1. Open Track Solo and choose the Tracks to hear (for example Drums and Lead).
  2. Soloed Tracks are overlaid on the waveform in colour.
  3. Clear all to go back.

You can see which Track you are hearing, so “this Track, right here” is easy to say.

06

Shared Notes right where you hear it

  1. While listening, tap Note here at the spot that caught your ear.
  2. Write and send; the Members receive it with the song position (Bar / Beat or minutes / seconds).
  3. Tap the note and Logic's Playhead on the Mac jumps to that position.

Leave notes like “widen the intro Pad a little” with the exact spot attached.

07

Check what to fix in Shared Notes

  1. Open the Shared Notes tab to see every note from the Members, with positions.
  2. Filter by Open, Done, Checklist and Applied.
  3. When a fix is in, tick Done. Who checked it, and when, is kept.

Nothing to fix is missed, and progress is visible at a glance.

08

Talk with Members in Messages

  1. Use the Messages tab to talk with the project's Members.
  2. Attach a Bar / Beat / Track position, or photos and videos.
  3. Tap a message with a position and Logic's Playhead jumps there.

Report and block from a long press on a message (right-click on the Mac).

09

See what Members are doing now

  1. The Members tab shows who is Online.
  2. Status such as Recording or Editing, and each Member's playback position, are shown.
  3. Update History (r1, r2, …) shows who updated the project and when.

Share the project's present, even when you are apart.

10

Available in 7 languages

  1. Choose a language at first launch: Japanese, English, German, French, Spanish, Korean or Portuguese (Brazil).
  2. Change it later under Settings › Language.
  3. Terms follow Logic Pro in each language (Track, Region, Playhead and so on).

Getting started

Install on your Mac, insert in Logic, pair your iPhone or iPad.

No complicated setup.

1
Install SYNCRA for Mac

Open the ZIP and move SYNCRA to Applications. It is notarized by Apple, so it opens right away.

2
Create an account

Sign in with Apple or use email. The 7-day free trial has no charge if you cancel during it.

3
Insert SYNCRA Monitor in Logic

Put the plug-in on Logic Pro's Stereo Out and Logic's audio is sent to your iPhone and iPad.

4
Pair your iPhone or iPad

In SYNCRA on the iPhone or iPad, scan the QR code or enter the 6-digit code shown on the Mac. Use the same Wi-Fi.

5
Listen, check, discuss

Move on the waveform, check with loops and solo, drop Shared Notes and finish the track with your Members.

FAQ

FAQ

What is SYNCRA?

A collaboration app that lets you hear the Logic Pro project on your Mac from an iPhone or iPad and review and fix it with your Members.

Can I use it on iPhone or iPad alone?

To hear audio you need SYNCRA for Mac (macOS 14 or later) and Logic Pro.

I cannot hear anything

Check that SYNCRA Monitor is on Logic's Stereo Out, that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, and that SYNCRA is allowed under Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on the iPhone or iPad.

Does the free trial turn into a paid plan?

Yes. When the 7-day trial ends it converts to a paid plan automatically. If you cancel during the trial you are not charged. If you cancel after the trial has ended, the charge for that period applies and renewals stop from the next one. The trial is once per person, up to 1 GB. If you cancel during the trial, cloud Shared Projects are deleted 30 days after it ends.

Cancelling and refunds

Cancelling stops future renewal charges. On iPhone and iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. See the refund policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Delete Account.
