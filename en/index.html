SYNCRA

Finish your Logic Pro tracks together. Listen to your Mac's audio on iPhone and iPad, and review and discuss changes on the spot.

Download for Mac

Features

Listen to Logic on iPhone and iPad
SYNCRA Monitor, inserted in Logic Pro on your Mac, streams uncompressed 32-bit float audio over the same Wi-Fi. Jump to any point from the waveform.
Loops and solo
Draw a loop on the waveform, store it as A, B or C, and recall it with a tap. Switch track solo from your iPhone or iPad.
Notes where you hear them
Add a shared note at the playback position. Tap a note and Logic's playhead on your Mac jumps there.
Talk with your members
Messages, shared checklist notes, and photo and video attachments keep everyone on the same page.

Getting started

  1. Download SYNCRA for Mac and move it to the Applications folder.
  2. Open SYNCRA and create an account (Sign in with Apple, or email).
  3. In Logic Pro, insert SYNCRA Monitor on Stereo Out.
  4. Open SYNCRA on your iPhone or iPad and pair using the QR code or 6-digit code shown on your Mac.
  5. Paid plans for the Mac app are purchased inside the app (payments are handled by Paddle).
Requirements
Mac: macOS 14 or later and Logic Pro. Version 0.1.2 (3), notarized by Apple.
iPhone and iPad: iOS / iPadOS 17 or later, on the same Wi-Fi as your Mac.
