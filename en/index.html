How it works

Getting started

1
Install SYNCRA for Mac

Open the downloaded ZIP and move SYNCRA to the Applications folder. It is notarized by Apple, so it opens right away.

2
Create an account

Open SYNCRA and sign in with Apple or create an account with email. The free trial lasts 7 days.

3
Insert SYNCRA Monitor in Logic Pro

Insert the SYNCRA Monitor plug-in on Logic's Stereo Out. Logic's audio is now sent to your iPhone and iPad.

4
Pair your iPhone or iPad

Open SYNCRA on your iPhone or iPad and scan the QR code, or enter the 6-digit code, shown on your Mac. Use the same Wi-Fi as your Mac.

5
Listen, review, discuss

Jump to any point from the waveform, repeat with loops and solo, leave a note on the spot, and talk it over with your members.

Using SYNCRA

How to use it

1
Listen to Logic

Audio arrives uncompressed (32-bit float). Watch the whole waveform and jump anywhere; skip 10 seconds back or forward, and return to LIVE in one tap. Switch between stereo, mono, left and right, and see the latency.

2
Store three loops

Draw a range on the waveform and store it as A, B or C; recall it with a tap. Loops follow Logic's cycle, so you can listen on repeat without touching your Mac.

3
Solo tracks

Switch track solo from your iPhone or iPad. Soloed tracks are overlaid on the waveform in color.

4
Notes where you hear them

Add a shared note at the playback position in one tap, by bar and beat or by minutes and seconds. Tap a note or message and Logic's playhead on your Mac jumps to that position.

5
Talk with your members

Exchange messages and shared notes with project members, and attach photos and videos. Notes can be checklists, so nothing to fix is forgotten. See who is online and where they are playing.

FAQ

FAQ

I cannot hear anything on my iPhone or iPad

Check that SYNCRA Monitor is inserted on Stereo Out in Logic on your Mac. Put your Mac and iPhone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi, and turn on SYNCRA under Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on the iPhone or iPad.

Can I use it on iPhone or iPad alone?

To hear audio you need SYNCRA for Mac and Logic Pro. An iPhone or iPad alone cannot play the audio.

Does the free trial turn into a paid plan?

No. The trial is 7 days, once per person, up to 1 GB of storage. If you do not subscribe, cloud projects from the trial are deleted 30 days after it ends.

Cancelling and refunds

Cancelling stops future renewal charges. On iPhone and iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. See Seller & Refunds.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Delete Account.
