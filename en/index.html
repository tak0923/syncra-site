Logic Pro × iPhone · iPad

Finish your Logic tracks
together.

Hear Logic Pro on your Mac in real time from an iPhone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi. Loops, solo and position-based Shared Notes are shared in real time, so review and fixes keep moving on the spot.

SYNCRA is a music collaboration app that connects Logic Pro on your Mac with iPhone and iPad.

Features

A new flow for reviewing your mix.

No need to crowd around the Mac. Hear the same audio, point at the same spot and settle fixes on the spot.

Share your Logic project in real time

Share the project you have open in Logic Pro with your members in real time. Playback position, loops, solo, position-based shared notes and messages reach everyone instantly. See who is online and where they are playing at a glance.

See how it works →

Play any specified spot automatically

Tap ▶ on a message with a position on your iPhone or iPad, and Logic on the Mac jumps to that bar and beat (or minutes and seconds) and starts playing. If the message names a track or region, it is selected too. Hear exactly the spot someone pointed to, without hunting for it.

See how it works →

Hear Logic on iPhone and iPad

SYNCRA Monitor, inserted in Logic Pro, streams uncompressed 32-bit float audio to your iPhone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi. Jump anywhere from the full waveform, skip 10 seconds, return to LIVE in a tap. Stereo / mono / left / right and latency display.

See how it works →

Store three loops

Draw a range on the waveform and store it as A, B or C; recall it with a tap. Loops follow Logic's Cycle, so you can repeat a section without touching the Mac.

See how it works →

Solo any Track

Switch Track solo from your iPhone or iPad. Soloed Tracks are overlaid on the waveform in color.

See how it works →

Shared Notes right where you hear it

Add a Shared Note at the playback position in one tap, by Bar and Beat or by minutes and seconds. Tap a note or message and Logic's Playhead on your Mac jumps there.

See how it works →

Talk with your Members

Messages and Shared Notes with photo and video attachments. Notes can be checklists so nothing to fix is missed. See who is Online and where they are listening.

See how it works →

Available in 7 languages

Japanese, English, German, French, Spanish, Korean and Portuguese (Brazil), using Logic Pro's own terms in each language.

01

Insert SYNCRA Monitor in Logic

How to insert it (3 steps)

1
Open Stereo Out in the mixer

In Logic, press X on the keyboard to open the mixer. (1) Find the “Stereo Out” channel strip and (2) the empty Audio FX slot.

  1. The Stereo Out channel strip
  2. The Audio FX slot (click here)
2
Click the slot and choose from the menu

Click the Audio FX slot and choose (1) Audio Units → (2) SYNCRA → (3) SYNCRA Monitor → (4) Stereo. From the second time on, you can pick SYNCRA Monitor from Recent Items at the top of the menu.

  1. Audio Units
  2. SYNCRA
  3. SYNCRA Monitor
  4. Stereo
3
Once inserted, the SYNCRA Monitor window opens

(1) “SYNCRA…” appears in the Audio FX of Stereo Out and (2) the SYNCRA Monitor window opens. (3) When it shows Waiting, you are ready.

  1. SYNCRA is inserted in Audio FX
  2. The SYNCRA Monitor window (Stereo Out)
  3. Waiting = ready
  1. Insert SYNCRA Monitor in the Audio FX of Logic Pro's Stereo Out.
  2. When it shows Waiting, you are ready. Open SYNCRA on your iPhone or iPad and it connects automatically on the same Wi-Fi, so Logic's audio arrives in real time (a number appears here only the first time for each iPhone).
  3. The audio path is Passthrough (no change to the sound): input goes back to Logic unchanged, sample for sample, and the same audio is sent to the iPhone. The sample rate and bit depth (32-bit Float) are shown.

Audio is sent only within the same Wi-Fi, never over the internet.

Parts of the screen

  1. ConnectionStatus such as Waiting or Connected, and how many iPhones and iPads are connected.
  2. SendingWhether audio is being sent to iPhone or iPad (Sending / Waiting). It starts automatically once connected.
  3. Audio formatSample rate, bit depth (32-bit Float) and the network round trip.
  4. Connect with iPhoneFor a new iPhone only, the same 6-digit number appears here and on the iPhone. If they match, press Match.
  5. Send levelLeft and right meters of the audio reaching this AU.
  6. Audio pathPassthrough: input goes back to Logic unchanged, sample for sample.
  7. Save and SendYou can also save and send changes to the Shared Project song in front from here.
  8. Same Wi-Fi onlyAudio is sent only within the same Wi-Fi, never over the internet.
More details (in-app guide)
Prepare your Mac
  1. Keep SYNCRA running.
  2. Insert the “SYNCRA Monitor” plug-in on Logic’s Stereo Out channel strip (from the Audio FX slot, choose “SYNCRA” under “Audio Units”).
  3. If macOS asks for “Local Network” permission, allow it (nothing is sent to the internet).

SYNCRA Monitor does not change the sound at all. It returns the input sound to Logic as is.

Connect on your iPhone
  1. In SYNCRA on your iPhone, open the “Monitor” tab. It automatically finds a Mac on the same Wi-Fi and connects.
  2. Only for the first connection to a Mac, the same 6-digit number appears on your iPhone and on the SYNCRA Monitor screen on your Mac. If they are the same, click “Match” on the Mac screen. If they differ, click “Doesn’t Match”.
  3. From the second time on, it connects automatically without a number.
What you can check on your Mac screen
  • “Connection Status”: States such as waiting and connected.
  • “Sending Status”: Whether it is sending to your iPhone.
  • “Connected to iPhone”: The connected iPhone and a “Disconnect” button.
  • “Send Level”: Left and right meters for the sound that arrived.

Playing, stopping, moving, looping, and exporting the whole-song waveform from your iPhone require that SYNCRA is running on your Mac and that Accessibility is allowed. While the waveform is being exported, Logic is brought to the front and operated. Be careful if you are doing other work on your Mac.

02

Register a Logic project

  1. Open Projects and press Add Logic Project. You can also drag a .logicx file in from Finder.
  2. Once added, you see its location, whether the Recording Folder (Media/Audio Files) was found, the date added and when it was last opened.
  3. Open in Logic launches Logic. Add Member lets you invite people right away.

Unregister only removes it from SYNCRA; your Logic project itself is not deleted.

Parts of the screen

  1. SidebarSwitch between Projects, Save and Send, Members, Account, History and Settings.
  2. Open Status PanelOpens the small status panel (SYNCRA Status) that sits next to Logic.
  3. Registered song cardStatus (Accessible / Needs update / Not accessible). Open opens it in Logic; ✕ removes it from the list (the file stays).
  4. Register a songDrag a .logicx from Finder into the dashed area, or choose Add Logic Project (⌘N).
  5. Add MemberInvite members to this song (QR code, invite code or SYNCRA ID).
  6. Seven tabsInfo, Messages, Checklist, Exchanges, Update History, Shared Notes and Members. New items show as red numbers.
  7. Project infoLocation, Recording Folder (Media/Audio Files), date added, last opened and Shared Project connection.
  8. ActionsOpen Status Panel, Open in Logic, Unregister (your Logic file is not deleted).
  9. How to use this screenOn every screen, the top-right button shows the steps for that screen.
More details (in-app guide)
How to add one (2 ways)
  1. Open “Project” in the list on the left.
  2. Method A: Drag a .logicx from Finder into the dotted frame on the screen (you can drag several at once).
  3. Method B: Click “Add Logic Project” (⌘N), choose the file, and click “Add”.

Only the file’s location is registered. The Logic file itself stays where it is.

Reading and using the cards
  • There are 3 states. “Accessible” means no problem. “Needs update” means the location may have changed. “No access” means you need to choose the location again.
  • “Open” at the bottom right of a card opens that song in Logic. Any other song currently open in Logic is closed. If there is unsaved work, Logic asks you to confirm.
  • The ✕ at the top right of a card removes it from the list on this Mac. The Logic file, the Shared Project on the server, and the members all remain.
The 7 tabs on the right
  • “Info”: Check the location, Recording Folder, date added, and so on, and use “Open in Logic” and “Unregister”.
  • “Messages”, “Checklist”, “Exchanges”, “Update History”, “Shared Notes”, and “Members”: These are explained on the following pages.
  • The “Messages”, “Exchanges”, “Shared Notes”, and “Members” tabs show the number of new items as a red number badge.
If it doesn’t work
  • “This project has already been added”: The same song cannot be added twice.
  • “Choose a Logic Pro project (.logicx)”: Choose a .logicx, not a folder.
  • “No access”: Choose the location again with “Choose Location Again” on the “Info” tab to fix it.

Status Panel: see Members in real time

  1. Press Open Status Panel to show a small status window.
  2. It lists the server connection and which Members are Online or Offline. Your own status is shared with Members in real time.
  3. While the Recording Folder is watched, starting a recording in Logic switches you to Recording automatically. You can also send a recording with Choose and Share Recording…

Change the panel's display size in Settings so it stays out of the way.

Parts of the screen

  1. ConnectionWhether you are connected to the server and sharing your status with Members.
  2. MembersName, @ID, role and status (Online, Recording, Editing, Syncing, Offline).
  3. StatusA yellow warning appears while someone is editing or recording.
  4. Recording Folder watchWhen you start recording in Logic, your status switches to Recording automatically.
  5. Choose and Share RecordingPick a recording and send it to Members. After a take, Share this take also appears.
  6. Drag to sendDrag a Logic Region or project (.logicx) onto the drop area. It is sent with its recording position.
More details (in-app guide)
Open it
  1. Click “Open Status Panel” on the “Project” screen.
  2. You can also open it from “Open Status Panel” on the “Info” tab or the same button in “Settings”.
  3. The panel is shown in front of Logic, and its position and size are remembered.
What you can see
  • “Members”: Name, @ID, role, and status (“Online”, “Recording”, “Editing”, “Syncing”, “Offline”). When someone else is editing or recording, a yellow notice appears.
  • Recording Folder monitoring: statuses such as “Monitoring the Recording Folder” and “Recording detected”.
  • “Messages”: The latest 5 and the input field.
  • “Received Audio”: audio members sent and its progress. When the song is in front in Logic, it is placed at its recorded position automatically.
Share a recording
  1. When a recording ends, “Share This Take” appears. Click it to share.
  2. With “Choose a Recording to Share…”, you can also choose and share other recordings.
  3. You can also send by dragging a Logic region into the send target on the panel (it is sent with its recording position information).
  4. A send that stopped partway can be continued with “Resume”.
Auto Sync switches (whole project)
  • “Send automatically when you save (⌘S) in Logic”
  • “Receive members’ newer versions automatically”
  • “Save and send when recording finishes”
  • “Ask whether to save and send after each edit or recording”
  • “Also reopen the open song as the newer version” (it waits if there is unsaved work)

All switches are on by default.

04

Messages with a position

  1. Open the Messages tab, type below and send.
  2. Enter a Bar and Beat, or minutes and seconds, under Position (optional) and the message arrives with that position.
  3. Press ▶ Bar / Beat in a message and the Playhead in Logic moves there. Attach photos and videos with the clip button.

The Track name is shown too, so everyone knows which part you mean.

Parts of the screen

  1. Message with a positionPress ▶ Bar / Beat and the Playhead in your Logic moves there.
  2. TrackShows which Track the message is about.
  3. Check □Adds ✅ and puts the message on the Checklist tab (kept only on this Mac).
  4. PositionEnter Bar and Beat, or minutes and seconds, before sending.
  5. Auto · Move on entryAuto fills in where you stop the Playhead in Logic. Move on entry moves the Playhead to the position you type.
  6. Region · GoRegion attaches a Logic Track and Region. Go moves the Playhead there before sending.
  7. Attach photos and videosUp to 10 per message (photos 50 MB, videos 1 GB). You can also drag them into the field.
  8. SendReturn sends (⌥Return for a new line). Up to 2,000 characters.
More details (in-app guide)
Send a message
  1. Open the project’s “Messages” tab.
  2. Enter your message in the field at the bottom and send it with Return (⌥Return for a line break). You can send up to 2000 characters.
  3. Choose photos and videos with the 📎 button, or drag them into the input field. Up to 10 per message; photos up to 50 MB and videos up to 1 GB. If there is an attachment, you can send even with an empty body.

“View Only” members cannot send photos or videos (they can send text).

Attach a position
  1. Enter a position in “Bar” and “Beat” (or “min” and “sec”) above the input field. Bar is 1–9999, Beat is 1–64, min is 0–1439, and sec is 0–59.
  2. Turn on “Auto” and when you stop the playhead in Logic, that position is entered automatically.
  3. Click “Region” to choose a track and region in Logic and attach them.
  4. Click “Move” and the Logic playhead moves to that position before you send.

When you click a received row that has a position, your Logic playhead moves to that position (Accessibility permission is required).

05

Checklist: confirm what needs fixing

  1. Tick the box on the right of a Member's message and it is collected in Checklist.
  2. When it is fixed, press Mark as Done and it moves to Done below.
  3. The date and time of each check are kept, so nothing is missed.

Return to Checklist puts an item back at any time.

Parts of the screen

  1. ChecklistMessages you ticked ✅ (kept only on this Mac; others can't see it).
  2. Mark as DonePress when finished; the item moves to Done.
  3. Checked itemThe original message (position, Track, text). Press the position to move Logic there.
  4. Checked atWhen you ticked it.
  5. DoneFinished items. Return to Checklist puts them back.
More details (in-app guide)
Checklist (your own list)
  1. Click the □ at the top right of a message row and a ✅ is added, and the message goes into the “Checklist” tab. Click it again to remove it.
  2. In the “Checklist” tab, move finished items to “Completed” with “Mark as Completed”. You can bring them back with “Back to Checklist”.

The message checklist is a record on this Mac only and is not visible to others (checks on Shared Notes are visible to all members).

What Checklist and Completed mean
  • ✅ (Checklist): things to act on later. Click the □ at the message’s top right to get ✅ and add it to the "Checklist" tab. Click again to remove it.
  • "Completed": when you have acted on it, click "Mark as Completed" in the "Checklist" tab. It moves to the "Completed" part of the same tab. "Back to Checklist" undoes it.
  • Only you see it: the message checklist is a record on this Mac only and the others cannot see it (checks on shared notes are visible to every member).
06

Shared Notes, shared in real time

  1. Notes with a position are shared in real time on the Shared Notes tab (Sharing in real time is shown at the top right).
  2. Enter a position and a note below, then press Add. The song's tempo (BPM and time signature) is shown as well.
  3. Filter by All / Open / Done / Checklist / Done (applied), press Go to move the Playhead, and mark Done once fixed.

A position can be given as Bar and Beat or as minutes and seconds.

Parts of the screen

  1. Sharing in real timeNotes reach every Member within seconds, and you see who is typing.
  2. TempoTempo and time signature used to convert positions. Press to change.
  3. FilterAll, Open, Done, Checklist, Executed.
  4. Number and positionNumber in position order, Bar and Beat, and who wrote it.
  5. ✅ and time✅ puts the note on everyone's Checklist. Written and updated times.
  6. Go · Return to ChecklistGo moves Logic's Playhead there. Ticked notes show Mark as Executed / Return to Checklist.
  7. DoneMarks the task as finished (visible to everyone). Reopen puts it back.
  8. PositionBar and Beat, or minutes and seconds. Empty means Bar 1, Beat 1.
  9. Write a noteType it and press Add (⌘Return).
More details (in-app guide)
Write a note
  1. Open the project’s “Shared Notes” tab.
  2. Enter a position (Bar and Beat), enter the content in “Note what caught your attention…”, and click “Add” (⌘Return). If the position is empty, the note is written at Bar 1, Beat 1.
  3. The note appears for all members within a few seconds. The names of people who are typing are shown too.
Working with notes
  • Click “Move” and the Logic playhead moves to that position.
  • Mark a note complete with “Done”, and bring it back with “Resume”. You can filter with “Open” and “Done”.
  • Click the ✅ (at the top right of a note) to add it to all members’ checklist. Filter with “Checklist”, and move items to “Completed” with “Mark as Completed”. You can bring them back with “Back to Checklist”.
  • With the trash can, you can delete only notes you wrote (they remain in the history).

Checks on Shared Notes are shared with all members. They are separate from the message checklist (yours alone).

Done, Checklist and Completed: what they mean
  • "Done": the note’s matter is settled. Everyone can see it. "Resume" puts it back to Open.
  • "Checklist": things to act on later. Tap the □ at the note’s top right to get ✅ and add it to everyone’s checklist. Tap again to remove it.
  • "Completed": when you have acted on a ✅ note, tap "Mark as Completed". If that was a mistake, "Back to Checklist" undoes it.
  • Filters: "Open" = not done yet, "Done" = done, "Checklist" = ✅ and not yet completed, and "Completed" = ✅ and completed.

On a ✅ note, the button changes to "Mark as Completed" / "Back to Checklist" (on a note without ✅ it is "Done" / "Resume"). All of this is shared with every member.

When edited at the same time

When the same note is edited at the same time, “Their version” and “Your version” are shown. Choose “Use Their Version” or “Use Your Version”.

Tempo setting

The tempo and time signature used to convert positions can be changed with the button at the top right of the screen, such as “120 BPM 4/4” (a constant tempo is assumed). For songs whose tempo changes partway through, the displayed positions may be off.

Update History and Auto Sync

  1. The Update History tab shows updates to the project.
  2. While Auto Sync is on, saving in Logic sends only what changed to Members, and Members' updates arrive automatically.
  3. When you invite a Member, the whole project is sent first. Press Refresh to check the latest.

You never have to send the whole project again each time.

Parts of the screen

  1. Auto SyncWhile on, saving in Logic sends only what changed, and Members' updates arrive automatically.
  2. RefreshLoads the latest versions (also refreshes every 20 seconds).
  3. VersionsVersions made by Auto Sync (r1, r2…). Check moves Logic to what changed in that version.
More details (in-app guide)
The “Update History” tab
  • Versions created by Auto Sync (r1, r2…) are listed newest first. The list refreshes automatically every 20 seconds.
  • Click “Check” to open that song in Logic, select the regions of the audio updated in that version, and move the playhead.
  • When there are many unchecked versions, you can mark them all as checked at once with “Mark All as Checked”.
  • “Place in My Song” on a member’s audio row places that audio as a new track at its recorded position in your latest song. After placing it, saving (⌘S) sends it.
The difference between “Update History” and “History”

“Update History” on the project screen is the list of versions of that song. “History” in the left list is a combined list of members’ actions across all projects.

  1. Open Save and Send on the left to decide whether changes to the song in front in Logic are sent to Members.
  2. If no Shared Project song is in front in Logic, a message says so.
  3. With the switch on, locking a Region’s SMPTE position sends only that Region’s audio, without asking (the project isn’t saved).

Send all other changes to the project with “Save and Send”.

Parts of the screen

  1. Save and SendDecide whether changes to the song in front in Logic are sent to Members.
  2. Song in frontShows No changes / Unsaved changes and so on, or that no Shared Project song is in front.
  3. SMPTE lock sends that Region onlyA Region whose SMPTE position you lock has only its audio sent, without asking (the project isn’t saved). Send other changes together with “Save and Send”.

How to lock the SMPTE position in Logic

Locking the SMPTE position is a Logic feature that fixes a region at a time measured from the start of the song (it stays at that time even if you change the tempo). In SYNCRA, locking a region sends only that region’s audio, without the confirmation pop-up (the project isn’t saved; members receive it in Exchanges and can place it where it was recorded. On by default; you can switch it with the toggle on the Save and Send screen above). Other changes are sent with “Save and Send”. After a recording you have 10 seconds to lock it.

1
Right-click the region and choose Lock

(1) Right-click the region you want to lock. In the menu that appears, choose (2) Lock SMPTE Position at the top. You can also select the region and press ⌘↓.

  1. The region to lock (right-click it)
  2. Lock SMPTE Position (⌘↓)
2
Once locked, a lock mark appears

A locked region shows a lock mark (1) to the left of its name.

  1. Lock mark = locked
3
To unlock

Right-click the locked region again and choose (1) Unlock SMPTE Position (⌘↑). While a region is locked, Lock SMPTE Position is grayed out.

  1. Unlock SMPTE Position (⌘↑)
More details (in-app guide)
The basic flow
  • SYNCRA checks the songs of Shared Projects every 6 seconds. When the save state changes and then stays steady for about 15 seconds, it sends only the parts that changed.
  • Members’ newer versions are checked every 20 seconds and received automatically.
  • A received version is assembled next to your song automatically. If it is not open in Logic, the switch happens right away; if it is open, the switch happens when you close it.
The confirmation pop-up
  1. When you edit a song in Logic and stop for about 2 seconds, “Save this change and send it to the members?” appears at the top center of the screen (it does not appear during playback or recording). After you stop recording, it reads “Save the recording and send it to the members?”.
  2. Click “Save and Send (Y)” to save the project in Logic and send every change not yet sent. Click “Don’t Send (N)” to neither save nor send (your work in Logic stays as it is).
  3. You can also answer with keys. Y or ← means “Save and Send”, and N or → means “Don’t Send”. Press a key on the same side twice to confirm. If you press the opposite side in between, it starts the choice over. esc means “Don’t Send” immediately; Enter and Space cannot be used.

For edits, the confirmation is asked only once, when the song becomes unsaved. After you choose “Don’t Send”, it is not asked again until you save. For recordings, it is asked for every take.

A region whose SMPTE position you lock is sent on its own, automatically

When you lock a region’s SMPTE position, only that region’s audio is sent, without a confirmation. The project is not saved (other changes are not sent). On members’ Macs it is placed at its recorded position automatically (it is listed in “Exchanges”). After a recording, you have a 10-second grace period to lock. You can switch this on or off with the switch “Send only a region whose SMPTE position you lock, without asking” on the “Save and Send” screen on the left (on by default).

The “Save and Send” screen in the left list
  • The state of the song in front in Logic is shown large, one at a time. While a confirmation is pending, the item in the list gets a light-blue ●.
  • When “There are unsaved changes” or “There are changes that haven’t been sent” is shown, you can always send every change to the project with “Save and Send”.

FAQ

What is SYNCRA?

A collaboration app that lets you hear the Logic Pro project on your Mac from an iPhone or iPad and review and fix it with your Members.

Can I use it on iPhone or iPad alone?

To hear audio you need SYNCRA for Mac (macOS 14 or later) and Logic Pro.

I cannot hear anything

Check that SYNCRA Monitor is on Logic's Stereo Out, that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, and that SYNCRA is allowed under Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on the iPhone or iPad.

Does the free trial turn into a paid plan?

Yes. The first 7 days of your first Master plan purchase are a free trial (once per person, up to 1 GB). Cancel during the trial and you pay nothing (on the App Store, at least 24 hours before it ends). Otherwise it switches to the paid plan when the trial ends. If you cancel after only the trial, cloud Shared Projects from the trial are deleted 30 days after it ends.

Cancelling and refunds

Cancelling stops future renewal charges. On iPhone and iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. See the refund policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Delete Account.
